The Challenge

The fashion industry continues to face a growing issue with fast fashion waste. The increasing consumption of clothing can contribute to environmental impact and encourages a cycle of buying and discarding clothes.

EcoFit was created to encourage people to make more responsible fashion choices while still being able to express their personal style.

The Solution

EcoFit is a mobile application designed to promote sustainable fashion by allowing users to buy recycled and preloved clothes while exploring diverse fashion inspirations.

  • Buy recycled and preloved clothing
  • Explore diverse fashion inspirations
  • Encourage creative and eco-conscious styling
  • Make more responsible fashion choices

By combining technology and sustainability, EcoFit aims to inspire users to express their individuality while making fashion choices that are better for the planet.

Our Impact

EcoFit supports the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals by promoting sustainable fashion practices and responsible consumption.

  • SDG 8 — Decent Work and Economic Growth
  • SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption and Production
  • SDG 13 — Climate Action

Through EcoFit, we strive to create a positive impact by helping people look good, feel good, and do good for the planet.
